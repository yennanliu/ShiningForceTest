# Asset credits

Every file in `assets/` is listed here with its author and license (doc/05 §8, doc/04 §4).
No file is ripped, traced or extracted from another game.

| Files | What | Author | License |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `audio/music_*.wav`, `audio/sfx_*.wav` | Placeholder chiptune music and sound effects, synthesized by `tools/make_audio.py` | Project Banner (generated) | Project-owned |
| `textures/units/*.png` | Placeholder map sprite sheets (48×64 frames, 9 × 4), drawn by `tools/make_art.py` from doc/05 | Project Banner (generated) | Project-owned |
| `textures/portraits/*.png` | Placeholder dialogue portraits (64×64 pixel art at ×4), drawn by `tools/make_art.py` from doc/05 | Project Banner (generated) | Project-owned |

When final art replaces a placeholder, keep the file name and size and replace its row with the artist's name and license.
